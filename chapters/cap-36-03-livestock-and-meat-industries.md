# Livestock and Meat Industries

CHAPTER 36:03 LIVESTOCK AND MEAT INDUSTRIES ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS SECTION 1. 2. 3.

Short title Interpretation Power to make regulations

Law 32, 1962, L.N. 84, 1966. An Act to provide for the control of the operation of abattoirs, slaughter houses, cold storages, canning plants, slaughter poles, bone meal factories, livestock produce store, tanneries and meat processing plant, for the control of dealings in and marketing, pricing, grading and inspection of livestock produce and the produce of tanneries and for matters incidental thereto. [Date of Commencement: 5th October, 1962]

### Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Livestock and Meat Industries Act.

### Section 2 — Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— "livestock" means such animals, birds, fish or reptiles as may be prescribed to be livestock for the purposes of this Act; "livestock industry" includes any trade in livestock or livestock produce or any manufacturing process of livestock produce; "livestock produce" includes meat, dry or fresh, or any other article whether processed or not, produced by or derived from livestock; "local authority" means—

(a) in the case of any township in respect of which a management board has been established, that management board;

(b) in the case of any other township or area, the District Commissioner of the district which includes that township or area; "plant" means any abattoir, slaughter house, cold storage, canning plant, slaughter pole, bone meal factory, livestock produce store, tannery, cannery or meat processing plant.

### Section 3 — Power to make regulations

(1) The President may, by statutory instrument, make regulations for the control of plant, and for the control of dealings in and marketing, pricing, grading and inspection of livestock produce and for anything required to be prescribed under this Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of the powers conferred by subsection (1) the regulations may—

(a) provide for the control of any plant;

(b) prescribe the structure, sanitation, drainage, ventilation and lighting of and the equipment, machinery, apparatus, instruments and utensils to be used in any plant;

(c) provide for the preparation, treatment, handling and marketing of livestock produce;

(d) prescribe the methods of slaughter of animals;

(e) provide for the inspection of meat and the fees that may be charged for such inspection and the marks to be placed on the carcasses; Copyright Government of Botswana

(f)

prescribe the standards of quality and grades of meat, the manner of grading, the fees which may be charged for grading and the marks to be placed on the carcasses to denote the various grades;

(g) prescribe the method of sale of livestock by live-weight;

(h) prescribe the method of handling, storing and transporting meat intended to be used by any person other than the producer thereof;

(i) prescribe the manner and form of application for the registration of plant;

(j) require local authorities, market masters, auctioneers, and persons engaged in the livestock industry to render returns or furnish information in respect of the livestock industry, and prescribe the form and manner in which such returns shall be furnished or rendered;

(k) prescribe the form in which records shall be kept by persons trading in livestock or livestock produce, or by the owners or persons having control of plant;

(l) provide for the registration of producers' agents at abattoirs, the fees to be paid for such registration and for fixing the commission which may be paid to agents at abattoirs;

(m) provide for the control of butchers' stalls and shops, and the sale of meat;

(n) prescribe the fees that may be charged by abattoirs for slaughtering livestock, and for cold storage;

(o) prescribe an export tax on any livestock produce.

(3) The President may apply any or all of the regulations to specified areas or specified plants.

(4) The regulations may provide that any person who contravenes any provision thereof or who fails to comply with any order, requirement or condition lawfully issued to or imposed on him by virtue of any regulation and with which it is his duty to comply shall be guilty of an offence.

(5) The regulations shall specify what penalty may be imposed for each offence thereby created and may also provide different penalties in the case of successive or continuous offences but no such penalty shall exceed a fine of P400, or, in default of payment thereof imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year, or such imprisonment without the option of a fine or both such fine and imprisonment.

(6) Any regulations made under this section shall be laid before the National Assembly within four days after they are made if the National Assembly is then sitting or, if the National Assembly is not then sitting, on the next sitting day thereafter.

(7) If within the next subsequent 21 days on which the National Assembly sits after any such regulations are laid before it, the National Assembly resolves that the regulations shall be annulled, the regulations shall thenceforth be void, but without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done thereunder, or to the making of any new regulations.
