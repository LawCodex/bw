# Ministerial Offices (Maximum Number)

CHAPTER 02:02 MINISTERIAL OFFICES (MAXIMUM NUMBER) ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS SECTION 1. 2. 3.

Short title Number of ministerial offices Number of offices of Assistant Minister

Act 18, 1967, Act 2, 1972, Act 16, 1977, Act 14,2002. An Act to determine the maximum number of offices of Minister of the Government of Botswana and the maximum number of offices of Assistant Minister which may be established under section 42 of the Constitution. [Date of Commencement: 1st September, 1967]

### Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Ministerial Offices (Maximum Number) Act.

### Section 2 — Number of ministerial offices

The number of offices of Minister of the Government of Botswana which may be established under section 42(1) of the Constitution shall not exceed 16.

### Section 3 — Number of offices of Assistant Minister

The number of offices of Assistant Minister which may be established under section 42(2) of the Constitution shall not exceed eight.
