# Acquisition of Francistown

CHAPTER 40:05 ACQUISITION OF FRANCISTOWN ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS SECTION 1. 2. 3.

Short title Areas of Francistown vested in State Areas of Tatitown vested in State Schedule A Schedule B Schedule C

Act 33, 1972, Act 2, 1975, Act 12, 1976. An Act to vest land in the Francistown Township in the State and for other matters incidental thereto. [Date of Commencement: 24th November, 1972]

### Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Acquisition of Francistown Act.

### Section 2 — Areas of Francistown vested in State

The ownership of all land in the Francistown Township described more fully in Schedule A hereto shall, other than land described in Schedule B hereto, vest in the State absolutely.

### Section 3 — Areas of Tatitown vested in State

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 2, the ownership of land described more fully in Schedule C hereto shall vest in the State absolutely.
